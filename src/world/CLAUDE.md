# src/world/

Conventions specific to city data, terrain, the road graph, and OSM import (`osm/*`). See the root `CLAUDE.md` for project-wide conventions.

- Cities are `CityData` (`src/world/cityData.ts`): `procCity.ts` generates one, `osm/import.ts` imports a real one, `cityBuilder.ts` renders it. URL: default `La Mariscal`; `?city=grid` or `?seed=N` for the generated city; `?hills=N` scales real elevation (default 1, true to life; `DEFAULT_HILLS`).
- Ground: `terrainHeight` interpolates the same two triangles per cell as the rendered terrain mesh and Rapier's heightfield (diagonal (r, c+1)–(r+1, c)); keep all three in sync. Road, sidewalk and junction surfaces are draped vertex by vertex over the terrain (`DRAPE` spacing), never kept level, or the ground pokes through (`tests/groundLayers.test.ts`).
- Coordinates for imported cities: x = east, z = south, meters from the bbox center. Heights are city-relative (lowest point in the city = 0). Anything placed in the world must use `groundHeightAt(city, p)`, never y = 0.
- `roadGraph.ts` handles any layout: polyline edges, one-way streets, intersection clustering, paved junction hulls, and `drivable` (largest strongly connected part, with map exits joined to entries). Cars leaving the map are recycled near the bus.
- Real bus lines: `data/raw/<zone>.routes.json` (Overpass route relations, `out body geom(bbox)`) is read by `build-city.ts` into `city.lines` (`src/world/osm/lines.ts`): each direction's path is chained from its ways, stops along it (facing its way) are kept in path order, both directions make a loop, lines sharing stops are merged.
- Rapid transit (`src/world/osm/stations.ts`, rendered by `transitBuilder.ts`): Trolebús/Ecovía/Metrobus stations come from the stop positions of their route relations (`data/raw/<zone>.stations.json` + `.routes.json`). Divided avenues have a gap between their one-way carriageways (the busway isn't imported): stations are islands in that median (measured by marching to the asphalt on both sides; two platforms only if it's wider than 11 m), else at the curb. Where OSM draws the carriageways too close for an island, `widenMedians` drops each one's inner lane along the station stretch (between the nearest junctions, tapered, outer curb kept) before anything else uses the roads; keep road pieces joined exactly when reshaping or the graph breaks. Their stops have `system` and `side: 'left'` (boarded from the left: the stop zone is to the bus's left, passengers use a left door). BRT lines only match their own system's stops, both directions from one relation (OSM often lists BRT ways/stops backwards); ordinary routes never use BRT stops. Metro de Quito entrances (`city.metro`) are canopies over stairs; `MetroCrowd` walks riders up and down them, spawning/vanishing only off-screen.
- Bridges and underpasses (`osm/grades.ts` at import, `elevation.ts`, `gradeBuilder.ts`): OSM `bridge`/`tunnel` ways keep their `layer`; where roads of different layers cross, the upper goes up or the lower down by 6.5 m. The lift ramps out along the network (10% grade, 16% where squeezed) but junctions and roads that must stay put are pinned at 0; crossings that can't get 4.5 m meet at grade instead. Roads stay locked level for 10 m around an at-grade junction (the graph trims roads back about that far; a ramp still climbing there high-centers the bus at the junction's edge), and the grade changes elsewhere are rounded off by relaxing the free vertices. Lifted roads come back resampled with `road.lift[]`; their original OSM vertices stay exact (never rounded), since other roads meet them at shared nodes and a rounded copy millimeters off silently cuts the junction out of the graph. Only the stretch of a road right at a crossing is pinned to street level (the rest may ramp), and ways continuing a crossed road count as crossed too; the stretches at a crossing are measured along the network, so a way split right at the crossing stays down (or up) under the other road past the joint instead of climbing while still under it. At runtime `roadProfiles` smooths the ground under them into a deck/floor height (a disc average by position, so two OSM ways meeting at a point get the same floor there: no step at joints); `surfaceY` makes decks, floors and the rendered road follow the ground's cross slope near ramp ends, where a level slab would leave a lip; the graph only joins roads at similar lift (`LEVEL_GAP`), and edges/nodes carry `y`/`lift` (`edgeY`). Traffic bodies (`laneSurface`, which also checks the solid surface near grades), the R reset (`snapToRoad(..., y)`: level weighs 4× sideways distance, with a clearance test against the static world), `Navigator.locate(..., y)` (same weight), the guidance arrow and the start pose all use those heights. Underpasses cut the terrain: `planTrenches` picks the cells, the heightfield is sunk there, and a 1 m patch rebuilds the ground, dipped down to the road floor inside the open cut (no edges to catch on), kept as a roof under streets crossing over it (or stacked above it in OSM) only where there's headroom (`HEADROOM`), open only at deep tunnel mouths over the asphalt. The ground is dug a cell's diagonal past each wall's inner face (`UNDER_WALL`, also beside a street alongside, never under its asphalt), so no patch triangle climbs in front of a wall (the old concrete "teeth"); a solid walkway (`APRON`, all faces drawn) covers the pit behind each wall that isn't under a roof, stopping at another road's asphalt; and a dark underlay at pit depth means no opening ever shows the sky. Patch cells, not vertices, decide the roof: a roof cell is flat at the ground, every other cell in the cut is floor (even corners owned by a ramp alongside at a higher floor), and roof edges get a vertical face down to the lower side (at most a 0.6 m lintel, never lower than `HEADROOM` over the floor), with railings only over the cut: no slivers or spikes at tunnel mouths, though a diagonal mouth's edge still follows the 1 m cell staircase. A ramp alongside near the top of its climb, more than `HEADROOM` over a cut's floor, covers it like a street (`coverTest`), and a shoulder/wall is only left out for another cut's floor at about ours (`otherFloor`). Retaining walls never stand across another road coming in partway down (a link branching off: an opening), nor on the lanes of a carriageway alongside that starts its ramp a little later (`rampAlongside`); a neighbor only shares the cut (no wall between) if its floor is near ours, so a ramp crossing over the cut near the top of its climb gets a wall under it, and it roofs the cut like any street; under a street at ground level drawn overlapping the cut they stop flush with it, as under a roof (leaving them out opens the cut's side: see-through ground you crash into). A street-level road overlapping a ramp only keeps its ground where that ground isn't above the ramp's asphalt (else it's a lip across the lane). Railings never stand on another road's asphalt, and a road alongside only cancels a bridge railing where its asphalt touches the deck (tested 0.15 m past the edge): two parallel decks with a slit between keep both railings. Every wall/railing collider is a convex hull of the drawn wall's own corners (`solidHull`, `stripCorners`), never a padded box, and every face of it is drawn (a buried back or end is in the open where a link splits off beside the cut), so nothing solid is invisible; junction slabs under decks are drawn too. Where two OSM ways of an underpass meet at an angle, each one's shoulders and walls reach `JOINT_OVERLAP` past its end into the next one's, and the wedge between their ribbons is paved (`nodeArea`), or the outside of the bend shows a slit to the sky. `e2e/underpassHoles.spec.ts` looks around from inside every underpass for any such hole. `tests/underpass/` (see its README) finds every bridge and cut (`catalog.ts`) and checks each legal passage: physics drive-throughs, traffic, `locate`/routes/R reset, swept bus volume, lips, drawn vs solid walls (`npm run test:underpass`; holes from the seat and chase camera in `e2e/underpasses.spec.ts`); bugs found are listed as known failures (`it.fails`): delete an entry when its fix lands. No stops, props, humps or sidewalks on ramps. `tests/mariscal.integration.test.ts` drives through the Patria/12 de Octubre underpass, both its links and the Puente del Guambra, and `tests/interchanges.test.ts` checks traffic uses the S→N link.
- Map edge: imported cities have `playArea` (bounds inset 70 m). Roadworks close it off (`boundaryBuilder.ts`: barriers, cones, "VÍA CERRADA", hoarding, one tall invisible wall). The invisible wall reaches below the deepest road floor along the edge, and barriers stand on each road level crossing it (down in an underpass too), or a bus drives out through a cut under the wall (`tests/edgeUnderpass.test.ts`). In the road graph nothing past it is drivable; the junctions just inside are exits/entries (with interior OSM dead ends) through the portal, and a car at an exit waits at the line until the player can't see it, then recycles. Stops are dropped at import unless a drivable lane going their way reaches them (`stopZone`). Pedestrian strips stop at the edge.
- Campus walls (`osm/campus.ts`, from `.areas.json`): universities, schools, hospitals, barracks get walls pushed behind the sidewalk; public streets through a campus stay open, walled on both sides. No gates. A wall breaks wherever its next piece would cross a street (a sample pushed past the wrong street at a corner made a wall across José Tamayo); their colliders are exactly the drawn height.
- `tests/lanesClear.test.ts` looks along every drivable lane on La Mariscal for anything solid in the way (walls, roofs, lips); keep it passing after any change to walls, cuts or decks.
- Far view (`backdrop.ts`): the sky, the real terrain 13 km around the zone (`city.horizon`, from a wider DEM window in `build-city.ts`) with landmarks (the Virgen del Panecillo, drawn 3.5× life size so she reads on the skyline; Cotopaxi, Antisana, Cayambe) render in a first pass with their own long-range camera (`farCamera` in `main.ts`), then the depth is cleared and the city renders over it with its own fog. Generated cities get the made-up mountain ring there instead. Anything added to the main scene covers the backdrop, so don't put sky or far scenery there.
- Buildings (`facades.ts`): the collider is the plain prism; everything on it is decoration with no collider (windows, balconies, cornices, awnings over the sidewalk, signs). Style comes from OSM `building` (kept as `Building.use`) and height: `civic` (campus, schools, hospitals, churches: no shops or balconies), `house` (≤ 3–4 floors, pastel paint by neighborhood from a slow position pattern), `glass` offices (≥ 5 floors on an "Av." avenue, or `office`), else `tower` (window rows split by piers, stacked balconies). Shopfronts only on walls whose outward probe reaches asphalt before another building; their sign is picked by OSM `shop`/`amenity` (`Building.shop`) when known. All variation is `hash2` of position: deterministic, no RNG.
- Shop signs are one canvas atlas (`signAtlas.ts`, 56 signs; add names there). Without a DOM (Node tests) there are no signs; nothing else depends on the DOM.
- Trees and lamps (`streetscape.ts`): OSM trees plus park trees scattered at import (leisure park/garden, landuse grass/recreation_ground, own seed so the rest of the import doesn't move), plus rows along sidewalks from `sidewalkSections` (avenues: trees and lamps both sides; side streets: lamps one side, trees on some). They keep clear of asphalt, junction hulls/crossings (4.5 m), stops, stations, Metro entrances, props, walls, lifted roads (10 m) and the map edge. Trunks and poles are solid (a cylinder the size of the drawn trunk/pole); crowns, arms and light are not. Instanced in 500 m tiles.
- Time of day (`sky.ts`, `LIGHTING`: day, sunset "Atardecer", night "Noche", a setting, `?tod=` overrides): fixed per session and baked when the city is built (`buildCity` reads it from the scene set up by `setupSky`; plain scenes are day). It sets sky, fog, haze in `backdrop.ts` (city lights on the far valley at night), share of lit windows (unshaded `glow` layer, merged with the sign atlas into one draw call per tile), lamp bulbs, light pools under lamps (additive draped decals, night only), traffic head/tail lamps and beams (`trafficView.ts`), and the player's one real `SpotLight` (`BusModel.setHeadlights`). No other real lights.
- Cost (this runs on phones): facade detail, glow/signs and streetscape tiles are wrapped by `withinRange` (an LOD that drops a tile past a distance: detail at 60% of the fog's far end, trees/lamps 75%, lit windows and signs all the way), so they cost nothing out of sight. Facade detail receives shadows but doesn't cast them, and walls against another building get no detail at all.
- OSM errors: `dropWays` in a zone (`tools/zones.ts`) removes ways before import (only for errors confirmed on the ground; La Mariscal drops none). At the Patria/12 de Octubre underpass all four mapped movements are real: 12 de Octubre N→S and S→N, the N→S turn through its tunnel onto Queseras del Medio, and the S→N link off 12 de Octubre through its own tunnel (under Queseras) up to Av. Patria (ways 24650066, 425195365, 420861081, 24650067; once dropped by mistake, restored after a local corrected it). Props and humps roll their dice per road segment (`spotSeed`), so adding or dropping a way only changes what's on it.
- Monuments (`monuments.ts`): famous ones the OSM extract lacks are listed per zone by their real lat/lon (`monuments` in `tools/zones.ts`, projected by `build-city.ts` into `city.monuments`) and modeled by hand: La Mariscal has the Universidad Central's Reloj Solar in Plaza Indoamérica, just past the west edge, in view from Av. América.

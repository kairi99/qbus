# src/world/

Conventions specific to city data, terrain, the road graph, and OSM import (`osm/*`). See the root `CLAUDE.md` for project-wide conventions.

- Cities are `CityData` (`src/world/cityData.ts`): `procCity.ts` generates one, `osm/import.ts` imports a real one, `cityBuilder.ts` renders it. URL: default `La Mariscal`; `?city=grid` or `?seed=N` for the generated city; `?hills=N` scales real elevation (default 1, true to life; `DEFAULT_HILLS`).
- Ground: `terrainHeight` interpolates the same two triangles per cell as the rendered terrain mesh and Rapier's heightfield (diagonal (r, c+1)–(r+1, c)); keep all three in sync. Road, sidewalk and junction surfaces are draped vertex by vertex over the terrain (`DRAPE` spacing), never kept level, or the ground pokes through (`tests/groundLayers.test.ts`).
- Coordinates for imported cities: x = east, z = south, meters from the bbox center. Heights are city-relative (lowest point in the city = 0). Anything placed in the world must use `groundHeightAt(city, p)`, never y = 0.
- `roadGraph.ts` handles any layout: polyline edges, one-way streets, intersection clustering, paved junction hulls, and `drivable` (largest strongly connected part, with map exits joined to entries). Cars leaving the map are recycled near the bus.
- Real bus lines: `data/raw/<zone>.routes.json` (Overpass route relations, `out body geom(bbox)`) is read by `build-city.ts` into `city.lines` (`src/world/osm/lines.ts`): each direction's path is chained from its ways, stops along it (facing its way) are kept in path order, both directions make a loop, lines sharing stops are merged.
- Rapid transit (`src/world/osm/stations.ts`, rendered by `transitBuilder.ts`): Trolebús/Ecovía/Metrobus stations come from the stop positions of their route relations (`data/raw/<zone>.stations.json` + `.routes.json`). Where a segregated busway runs beside a station (see the busway bullet) the platform sits on the busway: between two opposite one-way busways (the lens OSM draws as the station bay; `makeRoomAtBusway` pulls the pair apart with `shiftBusway`, tapering back to OSM's position, and the carriageways beside give up a lane or bulge, with `reshapeCarriageway`, so busway | platform | busway fits) boarded from the left from both, or on each side of a two-way busway (one stop per direction, a right-door curb stop: `side` absent). A lone one-way busway with no opposite direction keeps the old layout: divided avenues have a gap between their one-way carriageways: stations are islands in that median (measured by marching to the asphalt on both sides; two platforms only if it's wider than 11 m), else at the curb. Where OSM draws the carriageways too close for an island, `widenMedians` drops each one's inner lane along the station stretch (between the nearest junctions, tapered, outer curb kept) before anything else uses the roads; keep road pieces joined exactly when reshaping or the graph breaks. Their stops have `system` and `side: 'left'` (boarded from the left: the stop zone is to the bus's left, passengers use a left door). BRT lines only match their own system's stops, both directions from one relation (OSM often lists BRT ways/stops backwards); ordinary routes never use BRT stops. Platforms beside a busway are at most 24 m long. A shelter that a platform or the wider junction a busway makes would swallow slides along its street to the nearest clear spot instead of vanishing (a line loses no stop to a station). Metro de Quito entrances (`city.metro`) are canopies over stairs; `MetroCrowd` walks riders up and down them, spawning/vanishing only off-screen.
- Busways (`osm/busways.ts`, `Road.access = 'brt'`): the segregated BRT lanes are `highway=service` + `access=no` + `bus`/`psv` `designated|yes` ways (35 in La Mariscal: Ecovía on 6 de Diciembre, Trolebús on 10 de Agosto, Metrobus bits on Av. América; `isBusway`). They are roads named "Carril exclusivo", one lane (3.4 m) per direction (`buswayWidth`), appended after the general roads (indices of the general roads never move) and kept out of grade solving (street level only); `clipBusways` drops the stretches within 12 m of a bridge, underpass or ramp (and 30 m along a road from where it is off the ground), so structures are exactly what they are without busways; the graph never joins a busway to a road more than 0.5 m off the ground. Painted bus lanes on a shared carriageway (`bus:lanes`/`psv:lanes` on Av. América, 12 de Octubre, Universitaria) are NOT busways: they are one of an ordinary road's lanes and stay ordinary. OSM draws busways in the medians right against the carriageways, which are drawn with generic lane widths: `clearBusways` pushes each busway's interior points (at most 3.2 m, tapered, the ends and every vertex shared with another road stay exact) off the asphalt of ordinary roads, but never into another busway alongside: a one-way pair keeps a lane apart (each closes at most half the room between their lanes, and where OSM draws them closer, fanning out of a shared node, each moves half the shortfall away), or both carriageways squeeze the pair into one lane and oncoming ambient buses meet nose to nose (6 de Diciembre by the Casa de la Cultura); where the median is still too narrow they overlap (about a third of samples, a known limitation, `tests/busways.test.ts` caps it). Road graph: edges carry `access` and `brtDrivable`; `drivable` stays "strongly connected core of the GENERAL network" and is computed without busway edges (they never disconnect or swallow the streets; ordinary traffic, pedestrians' strips, sidewalks, humps, props, shelters' roads, the spawn and the R reset (`snapToRoad`'s `vehicle`) ignore them), while `brtDrivable` is the core of the whole graph (busways plus the streets they join), with the map portals as before. A node is `access: 'brt'` only when every road meeting there is a busway. `edgeAllows(e, cls)` is the one test; `Navigator(graph, 'general' | 'brt', generalCost)` filters by it (the default is the old behavior exactly; for 'brt', `generalCost` > 1 makes streets cost more per meter so routes keep to busways); rapid-transit routes (`RouteDef.system`) use the 'brt' class for stop location, legs and paths. A busway approach at a lit junction joins the phase of the nearest-axis ordinary approach (it never makes a phase of its own), and is unlit where no avenue runs beside it. Rendering: red-brown pavement in the same asphalt tiles (a hair above the streets'; not painted where its centerline lies on a street's asphalt, so red never covers lanes cars drive), white edge lines, a dashed yellow line between a two-way busway's lanes and "SOLO BUS" block letters every 90 m in the paint tiles (`busMarkings`): painted flush, no curbs or colliders, so nothing is solid to catch on. Trees, lamps, props, buildings and campus walls treat them as roads (asphalt); the map-edge barriers cross them like any road.
- Bridges and underpasses (`osm/grades.ts` at import, `elevation.ts`, `gradeBuilder.ts`): OSM `bridge`/`tunnel` ways keep their `layer`; where roads of different layers cross, the upper goes up or the lower down by 6.5 m. The lift ramps out along the network (10% grade, 16% where squeezed) but junctions and roads that must stay put are pinned at 0; crossings that can't get 4.5 m meet at grade instead. Roads stay locked level for 10 m around an at-grade junction (the graph trims roads back about that far; a ramp still climbing there high-centers the bus at the junction's edge), and the grade changes elsewhere are rounded off by relaxing the free vertices. The graph can merge junctions and trim a road further back than that (up to 15 m, more in a cluster): the import solves the grades again with every street-level junction's paved area that a ramp still climbs into kept level (`rampsIntoJunctions` → `separateGrades(roads, level)`, play area only), refusing any that would cost a crossing or fold a ramp into a V sharper than 16% over a bus length (`sharpestBend`): a long bus high-centers in that V (Queseras link at node 257 is the one refused). Lifted roads come back resampled with `road.lift[]`; their original OSM vertices stay exact (never rounded), since other roads meet them at shared nodes and a rounded copy millimeters off silently cuts the junction out of the graph. Only the stretch of a road right at a crossing is pinned to street level (the rest may ramp), and ways continuing a crossed road count as crossed too; the stretches at a crossing are measured along the network, so a way split right at the crossing stays down (or up) under the other road past the joint instead of climbing while still under it. At runtime `roadProfiles` smooths the ground under them into a deck/floor height (a disc average by position, so two OSM ways meeting at a point get the same floor there: no step at joints); `surfaceY` makes decks, floors and the rendered road follow the ground's cross slope near ramp ends, where a level slab would leave a lip; the graph only joins roads at similar lift (`LEVEL_GAP`), and edges/nodes carry `y`/`lift` (`edgeY`). Traffic bodies (`laneSurface`, which also checks the solid surface near grades), the R reset (`snapToRoad(..., y)`: level weighs 4× sideways distance, with a clearance test against the static world), `Navigator.locate(..., y)` (same weight), the guidance arrow and the start pose all use those heights. Underpasses cut the terrain: `planTrenches` picks the cells, the heightfield is sunk there, and a 1 m patch rebuilds the ground, dipped down to the road floor inside the open cut (no edges to catch on), kept as a roof under streets crossing over it (or stacked above it in OSM) only where there's headroom (`HEADROOM` = 4.2 m clear: the tallest bus plus suspension and pitch; roof-edge lintels never hang below it), decided over every cut at a point (`roofAt`, not the nearest one: that flips the roof cell by cell where two cuts meet, a cliff of ground in the tunnel). A street running alongside a cut doesn't roof it, except over its own asphalt (else the street has a hole), and no roof-edge railing stands on a street. Down in a cut (lift < -0.5) the shoulders are the cut's, never ground of a street alongside (that ground slopes over the curb lane); where a retaining wall stands under a street it's a patch cell's diagonal thicker with its top flush with the street, so the ground climbing up to the street stays inside it. Cuts widen on bends for a long bus's swept path (`bendAlong`, ≈ L²/8R along the chain of dug-in ways, ≤ 2 m), but never into a street's asphalt. Where two cuts share one (floors within `SHARED`) and the neighbor's floor is ≥ 0.3 m higher, a low wall flush with it faces the step. The ground is dug a cell's diagonal past each wall's inner face (`UNDER_WALL`, also beside a street alongside, never under its asphalt), so no patch triangle climbs in front of a wall (the old concrete "teeth"); a solid walkway (`APRON`, all faces drawn) covers the pit behind each wall that isn't under a roof, stopping at another road's asphalt; and a dark underlay at pit depth means no opening ever shows the sky. Patch cells, not vertices, decide the roof: a roof cell is flat at the ground, every other cell in the cut is floor (even corners owned by a ramp alongside at a higher floor), and roof edges get a vertical face down to the lower side (at most a 0.6 m lintel, never lower than `HEADROOM` over the floor), with railings only over the cut: no slivers or spikes at tunnel mouths, though a diagonal mouth's edge still follows the 1 m cell staircase. A ramp alongside near the top of its climb, more than `HEADROOM` over a cut's floor, covers it like a street (`coverTest`), and a shoulder/wall is only left out for another cut's floor at about ours (`otherFloor`). Retaining walls never stand across another road coming in partway down (a link branching off: an opening), nor on the lanes of a carriageway alongside that starts its ramp a little later (`rampAlongside`); a neighbor only shares the cut (no wall between) if its floor is near ours, so a ramp crossing over the cut near the top of its climb gets a wall under it, and it roofs the cut like any street; under a street at ground level drawn overlapping the cut they stop flush with it, as under a roof (leaving them out opens the cut's side: see-through ground you crash into). A street-level road overlapping a ramp only keeps its ground where that ground isn't above the ramp's asphalt (else it's a lip across the lane). Railings never stand on another road's asphalt, and a road alongside only cancels a bridge railing where its asphalt touches the deck (tested 0.15 m past the edge): two parallel decks with a slit between keep both railings. Every wall/railing collider is a convex hull of the drawn wall's own corners (`solidHull`, `stripCorners`), never a padded box, and every face of it is drawn (a buried back or end is in the open where a link splits off beside the cut), so nothing solid is invisible; junction slabs under decks are drawn too. Where two OSM ways of an underpass meet at an angle, each one's shoulders and walls reach `JOINT_OVERLAP` past its end into the next one's, and the wedge between their ribbons is paved (`nodeArea`), or the outside of the bend shows a slit to the sky. `e2e/underpassHoles.spec.ts` looks around from inside every underpass for any such hole. `tests/underpass/` (see its README) finds every bridge and cut (`catalog.ts`) and checks each legal passage: physics drive-throughs, traffic, `locate`/routes/R reset, swept bus volume, lips, drawn vs solid walls (`npm run test:underpass`; holes from the seat and chase camera in `e2e/underpasses.spec.ts`); bugs found are listed as known failures (`it.fails`): delete an entry when its fix lands. No stops, props, humps or sidewalks on ramps; humps also keep 25 m from any lifted point along the road (a car comes off a ramp top pitched up). On bridges, deck node slabs follow their roads' surfaces (a slab sunk below them leaves the next deck's edge as a lip), and where two carriageways' decks overlap at slightly different heights the higher one stops at the lower one's asphalt. `tests/mariscal.integration.test.ts` drives through the Patria/12 de Octubre underpass, both its links and the Puente del Guambra, and `tests/interchanges.test.ts` checks traffic uses the S→N link.
- Streets beside cuts (`osm/cutClearance.ts`, `clearCuts`, after the grades are solved): OSM draws a divided avenue's carriageways closer than our generic widths, so where one dives into an underpass and the other stays up their asphalts overlapped (12 de Octubre by El Ejido: 4 m between centerlines, 1.9 m of street hanging over the open cut, a hole buses dropped their wheels into). A street-level road running alongside an underpass (a road dug in deeper than 1 m somewhere; |cos| > 0.7) moves away from its walled stretch (lift under -0.3 m: distances are measured to that stretch only, since the wall is what the street must clear) until it clears the cut's floor and wall (asphalt halves + 1.1 m, plus the floor's bend allowance for a long bus's sweep, as `gradeBuilder` widens the cut, ≤ 2 m), at most 3.5 m, never toward another street, busway or cut within reach, tapered to nothing 14 m from every vertex it shares with another road (junctions stay exact). It stays put within 25 m of where it crosses over any way of that underpass (it's on the roof there; elsewhere a street crossing one part of a complex may run beside another: Ladrón de Guevara by the S→N link to Patria), where its asphalt overlaps the cut's by more than 2.5 m (a street running into the cut's mouth), and over the shallow top of a ramp (the cut there under 1 m deep) where it overlaps the cut's asphalt at all (the wall stands flush under it; a part-done push there leaves its ground, the terrain being carved to the nearest road, poking into the ramp's lanes). Ramp mouths (`dipMouths` in `osm/import.ts`, after the terrain is built): where a ramp still arrives below a street-level junction because leveling it was refused (the graph trims roads back further than the ramp has room to climb), the terrain around its mouth is lowered by its depth there + 0.5 m (full within 12 m, smoothstep out by 30 m) and every lifted road nearby is compensated so its own height doesn't move, except a ramp that would end up above the lowered ground follows it: the junction, its streets and sidewalks (draped over the terrain) dip to meet the ramp, as a real street does at a tunnel mouth. `build-city.ts` prints each one ("ground lowered 1.65 m around a ramp's mouth at (-63, 719)": the 12 de Octubre N→S link onto Queseras, a 1 m step and a walled trench inside the junction before). Measured at scale 1: with `?hills=` ≠ 1 the dip scales with the relief.
- Floods (`floods.ts`): `floodBasins(city)` groups the dug-in roads (lift under -1 m somewhere) whose floors come within their half widths + 1.5 m of each other at heights within 1 m (the Patria/12 de Octubre ways meet at junctions down in the cut, not end to end: grouped by ends only, their overlapping water stood at four levels), keeps the stretches less than 0.75 m (+0.3) over the group's lowest floor, and `FloodWater` draws them flat at that floor, the mesh raised by the flood depth (the ramps climbing out cover the rest). `floodedEdges` lists the graph edges down in each basin with their lowest point, for traffic. La Mariscal has 6 basins.
- Map edge: imported cities have `playArea` (bounds inset 70 m). Roadworks close it off (`boundaryBuilder.ts`: barriers, cones, "VÍA CERRADA", hoarding, one tall invisible wall). The invisible wall reaches below the deepest road floor along the edge, and barriers stand on each road level crossing it (down in an underpass too), or a bus drives out through a cut under the wall (`tests/edgeUnderpass.test.ts`). In the road graph nothing past it is drivable; the junctions just inside are exits/entries (with interior OSM dead ends) through the portal, and a car at an exit waits at the line until the player can't see it, then recycles. Stops are dropped at import unless a drivable lane going their way reaches them (`stopZone`). Pedestrian strips stop at the edge.
- Campus walls (`osm/campus.ts`, from `.areas.json`): universities, schools, hospitals, barracks get walls pushed behind the sidewalk; public streets through a campus stay open, walled on both sides. No gates. A wall breaks wherever its next piece would cross a street (a sample pushed past the wrong street at a corner made a wall across José Tamayo); their colliders are exactly the drawn height.
- `tests/lanesClear.test.ts` looks along every lane of every drivable edge on La Mariscal, at `DEFAULT_HILLS` and 1.3, at wheel, waist and roof height (0.6/1.5/3.3 m) for anything solid in the way (walls, roofs, lintels, lips); keep it passing after any change to walls, cuts or decks.
- Far view (`backdrop.ts`): the sky, the real terrain 13 km around the zone (`city.horizon`, from a wider DEM window in `build-city.ts`) with landmarks (the Virgen del Panecillo, drawn 3.5× life size so she reads on the skyline; Cotopaxi, Antisana, Cayambe) render in a first pass with their own long-range camera (`farCamera` in `main.ts`), then the depth is cleared and the city renders over it with its own fog. Generated cities get the made-up mountain ring there instead. Anything added to the main scene covers the backdrop, so don't put sky or far scenery there.
- Buildings (`facades.ts`): the collider is the plain prism; everything on it is decoration with no collider (windows, balconies, cornices, awnings over the sidewalk, signs). Style comes from OSM `building` (kept as `Building.use`) and height: `civic` (campus, schools, hospitals, churches: no shops or balconies), `house` (≤ 3–4 floors, pastel paint by neighborhood from a slow position pattern), `glass` offices (≥ 5 floors on an "Av." avenue, or `office`), else `tower` (window rows split by piers, stacked balconies). Shopfronts only on walls whose outward probe reaches asphalt before another building; their sign is picked by OSM `shop`/`amenity` (`Building.shop`) when known. Each layer of detail has its own distance off the wall (`OUT`): overlapping layers are never coplanar (they flicker), signs stand 10 cm out, and the shopfront (`shopBand`) stays under the first floor's windows/balconies or the cornice (one-storey shops: the fascia is the parapet, no cornice); window rows only as many as fit under the roof (`windowFloors`). The detail/glow/sign materials carry a small `polygonOffset` (`ON_WALL`) for far-away depth precision. `tests/facades.test.ts` builds every facade with recording builders and checks all of this; `e2e/facades.spec.ts` screenshots the free-roam spawn's signs. All variation is `hash2` of position: deterministic, no RNG.
- Shop signs are one canvas atlas (`signAtlas.ts`, 56 signs; add names there). Without a DOM (Node tests) there are no signs; nothing else depends on the DOM.
- Trees and lamps (`streetscape.ts`): OSM trees plus park trees scattered at import (leisure park/garden, landuse grass/recreation_ground, own seed so the rest of the import doesn't move), plus rows along sidewalks from `sidewalkSections` (avenues: trees and lamps both sides; side streets: lamps one side, trees on some). They keep clear of asphalt, junction hulls/crossings (4.5 m), stops, stations, Metro entrances, props, walls, traffic light poles (2 m), lifted roads (10 m) and the map edge. Trunks and poles are solid (a cylinder the size of the drawn trunk/pole); crowns, arms and light are not. Instanced in 500 m tiles.
- Time of day (`sky.ts`, `LIGHTING`: day, sunset "Atardecer", night "Noche", a setting, `?tod=` overrides): fixed per session and baked when the city is built (`buildCity` reads it from the scene set up by `setupSky`; plain scenes are day). It sets sky, fog, haze in `backdrop.ts` (city lights on the far valley at night), share of lit windows (unshaded `glow` layer, merged with the sign atlas into one draw call per tile), lamp bulbs, light pools under lamps (additive draped decals, night only), traffic head/tail lamps and beams (`trafficView.ts`), a glow around lit traffic light lamps after dark (`signalBuilder.ts`), and the player's one real `SpotLight` (`BusModel.setHeadlights`). No other real lights.
- Cost (this runs on phones): facade detail, glow/signs and streetscape tiles are wrapped by `withinRange` (an LOD that drops a tile past a distance: detail at 60% of the fog's far end, trees/lamps 75%, lit windows and signs all the way), so they cost nothing out of sight. Facade detail receives shadows but doesn't cast them, and walls against another building get no detail at all.
- OSM errors: `dropWays` in a zone (`tools/zones.ts`) removes ways before import (only for errors confirmed on the ground; La Mariscal drops none). `reverseWays` flips one-way ways drawn backwards (same rule): La Mariscal reverses 24652213, Av. América's northbound carriageway from the Universidad Central up to Fray Antonio de Marchena, reversed by mistake in OSM changeset 188174395 (2026-08-29; a playtester confirmed it runs north). Divided avenues are checked: `sameWayStretches` (`osm/carriageways.ts`) finds stretches where both carriageways of a named avenue run the same way (branches that split off or merge are left out), `build-city.ts` warns about any in the play area, `npx tsx tools/carriageways.ts` lists every avenue's carriageway directions, and `tests/carriageways.test.ts` keeps them opposite. Humps never reach onto another road's lanes (a link merging into an avenue). At the Patria/12 de Octubre underpass all four mapped movements are real: 12 de Octubre N→S and S→N, the N→S turn through its tunnel onto Queseras del Medio, and the S→N link off 12 de Octubre through its own tunnel (under Queseras) up to Av. Patria (ways 24650066, 425195365, 420861081, 24650067; once dropped by mistake, restored after a local corrected it). Props and humps roll their dice per road segment (`spotSeed`), so adding or dropping a way only changes what's on it.
- Traffic signals: `fetch-osm.ts` gets `highway=traffic_signals` nodes in the `osm` extract; `importSignals` keeps those on a drivable road (within 2 m; pedestrian-only `traffic_signals=crossing*` values dropped) as `city.signals`, each with the `lift` of the road it's on (a light in an underpass isn't the street's over it). Which junction a signal lights is decided at run time from the road graph (`gameplay/trafficLights.ts`), since OSM often tags each approach's stop line a few meters out instead of the junction. Generated cities light every crossing of an avenue away from the grid's edge.
- Monuments (`monuments.ts`): famous ones the OSM extract lacks are listed per zone by their real lat/lon (`monuments` in `tools/zones.ts`, projected by `build-city.ts` into `city.monuments`) and modeled by hand: La Mariscal has the Universidad Central's Reloj Solar in Plaza Indoamérica, just past the west edge, in view from Av. América.

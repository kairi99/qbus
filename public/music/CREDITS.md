# Radio music credits

The three songs on the bus radio are **original compositions made for QBus**: melodies,
harmonies, rhythms and sounds were written and synthesized from scratch by the code in
`tools/music/` (pure synthesis: no samples, no recordings, no AI music services, no melodies,
lyrics or riffs taken from existing songs). They are released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain
dedication), so they can be reused freely.

The bands, stations and DJs are fictional.

| File | Station | Song | "Artist" | Genre |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sanjuanito.mp3` | Radio Sanjuanito 98.5 | Guambrita del Panecillo | Los Chullas del Ejido | San Juanito (Ecuadorian Andean) |
| `chicha.mp3` | La Buseta 101.3 | Cumbia del Trole Perdido | Chichero Andrade y su Combo Interparroquial | Chicha (Peruvian/Andean cumbia) |
| `reggaeton.mp3` | Perreo FM 94.0 | Perreo en la Ecovía | Lil Ñaño ft. DJ Mitad del Mundo | Reggaeton |

Regenerate with `python3 tools/music/render.py` (see `tools/music/README.md`).

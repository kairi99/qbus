# Radio music credits

The six songs on the bus radio (two per station) are **original compositions made for QBus**: melodies,
harmonies, rhythms and sounds were written and synthesized from scratch by the code in
`tools/music/` (pure synthesis: no samples, no recordings, no AI music services, no melodies,
lyrics or riffs taken from existing songs). They are released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain
dedication), so they can be reused freely.

The bands, stations and DJs are fictional.
